Safety Tips

Mind the gap

Step carefully while boarding. Stand behind the yellow line until the train fully stops.

Watch your belongings

Keep bags in front of you during peak hours. Report unattended luggage immediately.

No eating or drinking

Consuming food or beverages inside the train is prohibited and carries a fine.

Priority seating

Seats near doors are reserved for elderly, pregnant, and Divyangjan passengers.

Emergency helpline

Use the Talk-Back button in the coach or call NCRTC helpline 1800-209-0200. Station Control Room available 24/7.

No photography

Photography inside trains, platforms, or restricted areas is strictly prohibited.
