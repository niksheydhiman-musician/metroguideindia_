Key Facilities

  • First Aid — Station Control Room
  • Washrooms — Inside paid concourse, near Station Control Room
  • Drinking Water — Inside paid concourse, near Station Control Room
  • Divyangjan Friendly
  • Premium Lounge — Available on both platforms (Meerut end)

Exit Gates

  • Gate 1 (Open) — On road going to Chaudhary Charan Singh Marg
  • Gate 2 (Open) — On road going towards Anand Vihar Terminal Railway Station
  • Gate 3 (Open) — Connected to FOB towards DMRC and Anand Vihar ISBT

Platforms

  • Platform 1 (Open) — Towards Meerut · Premium Lounge at Meerut end
  • Platform 2 (Open) — Towards Delhi · Premium Lounge at Meerut end

Nearby Transit & Landmarks

  • Pacific Mall · Vivek Vihar · Karkardooma · Shreshtha Vihar
  • NH-9 (Delhi–Meerut Expressway) & Ghaziabad Link Road

Bus Connections

Safety Tips

Mind the gap

Step carefully while boarding. Stand behind the yellow line until the train fully stops.

Watch your belongings

Keep bags in front of you during peak hours. Report unattended luggage immediately.

No eating or drinking

Consuming food or beverages inside the train is prohibited and carries a fine.

Priority seating

Seats near doors are reserved for elderly, pregnant, and Divyangjan passengers.

Emergency helpline

Use the Talk-Back button in the coach or call NCRTC helpline 1800-209-0200. Station Control Room: 9289928746.

No photography

Photography inside trains, platforms, or restricted areas is strictly prohibited.
