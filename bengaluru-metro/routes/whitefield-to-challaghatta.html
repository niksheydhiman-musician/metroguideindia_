Step 2 · No interchange needed

Majestic · Stay on board

This is a direct Purple Line ride with 0 interchanges. At Majestic you may switch to the Green Line (Level 2 ⇄ Level 1) for other trips, but do not alight for this journey.

Step 3 · Arrive at Challaghatta

Purple Line · Terminal Platform

The train terminates here, so everyone alights. Use Gate A (Mysore Road, RR Medical College) or Gate B (Kengeri NICE Road junction) to leave the station.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the metro take from Whitefield to Challaghatta?

The direct Purple Line journey covers 43.5 km across 37 stations in about 82 minutes, with no interchange.

What is the fare from Whitefield to Challaghatta?

The token fare is ₹90. Smart Card and WhatsApp QR travellers pay ₹85.5 at peak (5% off) and ₹81.0 off-peak (10% off).

Do I need to change trains between Whitefield and Challaghatta?

No. Both stations are on the Purple Line, so the journey is direct. Stay on the train through Majestic.

What are the first and last train timings?

Trains run 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM on weekdays and 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM on Sundays. Peak frequency is every 3–5 minutes and off-peak every 8–10 minutes.

Which platform do I board at Whitefield?

Board Platform 2 at Whitefield (Kadugodi) for trains towards Challaghatta.
