Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the journey from Lalbagh Botanical Garden to Whitefield (Kadugodi) take?

The metro portion covers 31.2 km in about 57 minutes, estimated at two minutes per stop plus interchange walks. Allow extra time for waiting.

What is the token and Smart Card fare from Lalbagh Botanical Garden to Whitefield (Kadugodi)?

The token fare for the metro portion is ₹70; Smart Card and WhatsApp QR fare is ₹66.5 at peak (5% off) and ₹63 off-peak (10% off). Off-peak hours are 05:00–08:00, 11:00–16:30 and 20:00–23:00, and all day Sunday.

Where do I change platforms?

Allow about 5 minutes. Follow signs from Underground Level 1 (Green Line) to Underground Level 2 (Purple Line); board Platform 1 towards Whitefield (Kadugodi).

What are the first and last train timings?

Network service runs 05:00 AM to 11:00 PM Monday to Saturday, and 07:00 AM to 11:00 PM on Sundays and holidays. Confirm first and last departures at your station.

Which exit gates serve Whitefield (Kadugodi)?

Gate 1: Whitefield Railway Station & Main Entrance; Gate 2: Kadugodi Bus Stand & Feeder Bus Bay.
