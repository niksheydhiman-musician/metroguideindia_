Frequently Asked Questions

About 48 minutes (42–55 minutes with waiting): roughly 7 minutes by Meerut Metro to Shatabdi Nagar, a short interchange, then about 30 minutes on Namo Bharat to Ghaziabad.
₹120 in a standard coach and ₹140 in premium for the 42.9 km trip. Paying with NCMC or a Namo Bharat app QR ticket gives a 10% discount, about ₹108 standard or ₹126 premium.
Change at Shatabdi Nagar, which has both Meerut Metro and Namo Bharat platforms. Begumpul, one metro stop the other way, is an alternative. One integrated ticket covers both.
The first Meerut Metro from Bhaisali toward Meerut South is about 6:21 AM and Namo Bharat runs 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, starting at 8:00 AM on Sundays. Leave Bhaisali by about 9:30 PM to be safe.
Gate 3 faces Shaheed Sthal but was reported closed at last check. Use Gate 2 or Gate 5 (Meerut end) and walk about 250 m; confirm on the station signage.
