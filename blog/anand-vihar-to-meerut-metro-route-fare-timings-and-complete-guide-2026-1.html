Anand Vihar to Meerut Metro: Route, Fare, Timings and Complete Guide (2026)

MetroGuideIndia · 2026-11-25

If you want to go from Anand Vihar to Meerut by metro, you take the Namo Bharat train from Anand Vihar station. It is the fastest and most comfortable way to reach Meerut. You do not need to change trains to reach Meerut South, Shatabdi Nagar, Begumpul or Modipuram. The ride takes about 35 to 50 minutes, depending on the station and the train type.

This guide covers the route, fares, timings, the new fast trains, every Meerut station, and the bus and train alternatives.

Is there a direct metro from Anand Vihar to Meerut?

Yes. Since February 2026, the full Delhi to Meerut corridor has been open. Namo Bharat trains run from Sarai Kale Khan in Delhi to Modipuram in Meerut, covering about 82 km. Anand Vihar is the third station on this line.

You can board at Anand Vihar and ride straight into Meerut. There is no need to go to Ghaziabad first or take a bus.

Namo Bharat, RapidX and Meerut Metro: what is the difference?

People use three names for the same system, and this causes confusion.

  • RapidX was the old name of the project. Many people still search for "rapid metro from Anand Vihar to Meerut."
  • Namo Bharat is the current name of the regional train. It connects Delhi, Ghaziabad and Meerut.
  • Meerut Metro is the city metro inside Meerut. It runs between Meerut South and Modipuram and shares some stations with Namo Bharat.

If you are going from Anand Vihar to Meerut, you start on a Namo Bharat train. If your final stop is a city station such as Meerut Central, you may need to continue on Meerut Metro. The route pages linked below explain this for each station.

Anand Vihar to Meerut Metro route: step by step

Step 1: Enter Anand Vihar Namo Bharat station.

The station is underground. Use the exit gate that matches where you are coming from (see the next section).

Step 2: Buy your ticket.

You can use the Namo Bharat app, an NCMC card, UPI, or a single-journey token from the counter. Cash works at the counter.

Step 3: Go to the platform for Meerut.

Follow the signs for Modipuram. That is the last station towards Meerut.

Step 4: Check the train type.

Regular trains stop at every station. Fast trains stop at only five stations. Check the display board and listen to announcements before you board.

Step 5: Ride and get down at your station.

Your ride passes Sahibabad, Ghaziabad, Guldhar, Duhai, Duhai Depot, Murad Nagar, Modi Nagar South, Modi Nagar North, Meerut South, Shatabdi Nagar, Begumpul and Modipuram, in that order.

Step 6: Exit and continue.

Pick the right exit gate and take an auto, e-rickshaw or cab for the last stretch.

How to reach Anand Vihar Namo Bharat station

Anand Vihar is built to connect with other transport.

  • Delhi Metro: Take the Blue Line or Pink Line to Anand Vihar ISBT. Use Gate 3, which links to the Namo Bharat station through a foot over bridge.
  • Anand Vihar ISBT (bus terminal): Use Gate 3 as well.
  • Anand Vihar railway terminal: Use Gate 2, on the railway terminal side.
  • By road: Use Gate 1, on Chaudhary Charan Singh Marg.
  • By city bus: Routes D 035 and D 039 run from Anand Vihar ISBT every 20 minutes.

All three gates have lifts and escalators. For full station details, see our Anand Vihar Namo Bharat station guide.

Driving to the station? Parking is available. Four-wheelers pay ₹50 for up to 6 hours and ₹100 for a full day. Two-wheelers pay ₹20 for up to 6 hours. Pick-up and drop-off for 10 minutes is free.

Premium coach: It costs about 20% more than the standard fare. It has wider seats, more legroom and space for luggage.

Children: Children under 90 cm in height travel free.

Daily travellers: A return trip to Meerut South costs about ₹260 in standard class. Check the Namo Bharat app or NCMC offers for savings.

Fares can change. Always confirm the final amount in the Namo Bharat app or at the station counter before you travel.

Timings and the new fast trains

Regular service

  • Monday to Saturday: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM
  • Sunday: 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM
  • Frequency: a train every 10 minutes

New: fast Namo Bharat trains (from 16 November 2026)

NCRTC added 8 limited-stop trains during peak hours. They stop only at Sarai Kale Khan, Anand Vihar, Ghaziabad, Begumpul and Modipuram.

  • Morning: departures at 8:00 AM and 8:30 AM from both ends
  • Evening: departures at 6:00 PM and 6:30 PM from both ends
  • Anand Vihar to Ghaziabad: about 8 minutes
  • Anand Vihar to Begumpul: about 36 minutes
  • Full corridor: under 50 minutes

These trains run alongside the regular services, so the 10-minute frequency stays the same.

Which one should you take? If you are going to Begumpul or Modipuram during peak hours, the fast train saves time. If you are going to Meerut South or Shatabdi Nagar, take the regular train, because the fast train does not stop there.

Anand Vihar to Meerut distance

  • By Namo Bharat: about 39 km from Anand Vihar to Meerut South.
  • By road: roughly 65 to 70 km, depending on where in Meerut you stop.
  • Time by road: often 1.5 to 2.5 hours, because of traffic on the Delhi to Meerut stretch.
  • Time by Namo Bharat: about 35 to 50 minutes.

The train is shorter in distance and far more predictable in time. Traffic does not affect it.

Anand Vihar to Meerut by bus

Buses leave from Anand Vihar ISBT, right next to the Namo Bharat station.

  • Fare: roughly ₹100 to ₹150 depending on the bus type. Confirm at the counter or on the operator's app.
  • Time: more than an hour in good traffic, often much longer at peak hours.
  • Best for: travellers going to a part of Meerut that is far from any metro station, or those carrying heavy luggage who want a single ride.

Anand Vihar to Meerut by train (Indian Railways)

Regular trains also connect Delhi, Ghaziabad and Meerut. Anand Vihar Terminal is an Indian Railways station next to the ISBT. Tickets cost less than Namo Bharat, but the journey is slower and train availability depends on the day. Check IRCTC for the current list.

Coming from a Meerut railway station? If you are at Meerut Cantt or Meerut City station and want the metro, take an auto, e-rickshaw or cab to the nearest Namo Bharat station, usually Meerut South or Begumpul. Check the route page below before you leave.

Meerut to Anand Vihar metro (return journey)

The route is the same in reverse. Board towards Sarai Kale Khan and get down at Anand Vihar.

  • Meerut South to Anand Vihar
  • Shatabdi Nagar to Anand Vihar
  • Begumpul to Anand Vihar
  • Modipuram to Anand Vihar
  • Meerut Central to Anand Vihar

Fares are the same in both directions. For the full list of stations, read our Complete Namo Bharat RRTS Travel Guide (2026).

Tips for a smooth trip

  • Avoid peak hours if you can. Mornings from 7:00 to 9:30 AM and evenings from 4:30 to 7:00 PM are the busiest.
  • Use the Namo Bharat app or NCMC to skip the ticket queue.
  • Check the train type before boarding, so you do not end up on a fast train that skips your station.
  • Stand behind the yellow line and mind the gap when boarding.
  • Keep bags in front of you in crowded trains.
  • Do not eat or drink inside the train. It carries a fine.
  • Need help? Call the NCRTC helpline at 1800-209-0200.

Final thoughts

Anand Vihar to Meerut by Namo Bharat is simple. Board at Anand Vihar, ride towards Modipuram, and get down at the Meerut station closest to your destination. You save time, avoid traffic and travel in comfort.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a metro from Anand Vihar to Meerut?

Yes. The Namo Bharat train runs from Anand Vihar to Meerut. It reaches Meerut South in about 35 minutes and Modipuram in about 50 minutes.

What is the fare from Anand Vihar to Meerut by metro?

The standard fare is ₹130 to Meerut South, ₹140 to Shatabdi Nagar, ₹160 to Begumpul and ₹180 to Modipuram. Premium coaches cost about 20% more.

How long does it take to go from Anand Vihar to Meerut by metro?

About 35 minutes to Meerut South. The new fast train reaches Begumpul in about 36 minutes. A regular train to Modipuram takes roughly 50 minutes.

What is the first and last Namo Bharat train from Anand Vihar?

Service runs from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM from Monday to Saturday and 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on Sunday. Check the app for exact departure times.

Is Anand Vihar to Meerut metro direct?

Yes, for Meerut South, Shatabdi Nagar, Begumpul and Modipuram. For Meerut Metro city stations like Meerut Central, you may need to continue on Meerut Metro. See the station route pages.

What is the difference between rapid metro and Namo Bharat?

They are the same system. RapidX was the earlier name. Namo Bharat is the current name.

What is the distance from Anand Vihar to Meerut?

About 39 km by Namo Bharat to Meerut South and roughly 65 to 70 km by road.

How do I go from Anand Vihar ISBT to the Namo Bharat station?

Use Gate 3. A foot over bridge connects the ISBT and Delhi Metro to the Namo Bharat station.

Is there a bus from Anand Vihar to Meerut?

Yes. Buses leave from Anand Vihar ISBT. They are cheaper than the train but take much longer.

Which is better, bus or Namo Bharat?

Namo Bharat is faster, has no traffic delays and has a fixed schedule. A bus costs a little less but can take twice as long or more.

Can I pay by UPI at Anand Vihar?

Yes. You can pay with UPI, the Namo Bharat app, NCMC or a token from the counter.

Is parking available at Anand Vihar Namo Bharat station?

Yes. Four-wheelers pay ₹50 for up to 6 hours. Two-wheelers pay ₹20. The first 10 minutes for pick-up and drop-off are free.
