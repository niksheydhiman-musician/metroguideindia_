Anand Vihar to Ghaziabad Distance: Overview of the Journey

Before diving into the route maps, let's look at the foundational numbers. The geographic distance from Ghaziabad to Anand Vihar varies depending on your specific destination in Ghaziabad (such as Kaushambi, Vaishali, Sahibabad, Mohan Nagar, or the old city centre) and your chosen mode of transportation.

  • By Road: The road distance from Anand Vihar ISBT/Terminal to central Ghaziabad is approximately 13 km to 16.4 km, depending on the exact route taken via Madan Mohan Malviya Marg or Link Road.
  • By Namo Bharat RRTS: The direct track distance between Anand Vihar RRTS station and Ghaziabad RRTS station is exactly 10.7 km.
  • By Delhi Metro Network: The distance ranges between 3 km and 12 km depending on whether your stop is just across the border (like Vaishali) or further inside the city (like Shaheed Sthal).
🇮🇳 Hindi Quick Answer — Anand Vihar se Ghaziabad kitna kilometre hai? Anand Vihar se Ghaziabad ki sadak ki duri lagbhag 14–16 km hai, jabki naye Namo Bharat Rapid Rail se yeh duri keval 10.7 km hai. Agar aap Vaishali Metro ja rahe hain, toh yeh keval 3 km ki duri par hai.

Option 1: The Blue Line Extension (Direct to Vaishali)

If your destination is near Kaushambi, Vaishali, or parts of Indirapuram, the Blue Line is your fastest traditional metro route.

  • Boarding Station: Anand Vihar ISBT (Platform 1)
  • Line: Blue Line (Branch towards Vaishali)
  • Intermediate Station: Kaushambi
  • Terminal Station: Vaishali (Ghaziabad)
  • Travel Time: ~6 to 8 minutes
  • Fare: ₹10 to ₹20
💡
Pro TipBoard the Blue Line towards Dwarka/Noida at Anand Vihar ISBT, then take the Vaishali branch — the platform split is clearly signed inside the station.

Option 2: The Red Line Route (To Shaheed Sthal / New Bus Adda)

If you need to reach central Ghaziabad, Mohan Nagar, Arthala, or the Hindon River region, an interchange via the Red Line is necessary.

  1. Board the Pink Line at Anand Vihar towards Shiv Vihar.
  2. De-board at Welcome Metro Station and switch to the Red Line heading towards Shaheed Sthal (New Bus Adda).
  3. This route brings you directly into the heart of Ghaziabad city, passing Raj Bagh, Mohan Nagar, and Shyam Park.
  • Travel Time: ~40 to 45 minutes
  • Fare: ₹40 to ₹50

The Game Changer: Namo Bharat High-Speed RRTS

For those looking to bypass city traffic and standard metro stops altogether, the Delhi–Ghaziabad–Meerut RRTS corridor has completely transformed the transit infrastructure between these two hubs.

The Anand Vihar Namo Bharat Station is seamlessly integrated underground with Anand Vihar Indian Railway Terminal, Delhi Metro (Blue/Pink Lines), and both Delhi and Kaushambi ISBTs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Anand Vihar se Ghaziabad kitne kilometre hai?
Agar aap road se ja rahe hain toh Anand Vihar se Ghaziabad central ki duri lagbhag 14 se 16 kilometre hai. Wahi agar aap Vaishali (jo ki Ghaziabad ka hissa hai) ja rahe hain, toh wo Anand Vihar se keval 3 kilometer ki duri par sthit hai.
What is the first and last metro train timing from Anand Vihar to Vaishali?
The first Blue Line metro train towards Vaishali leaves Anand Vihar at 06:13 AM, and the last scheduled train departs at 10:33 PM. Always check the DMRC official app for the most current schedule.
Can I use my Delhi Metro Smart Card on the Namo Bharat RRTS train?
Integrated smart transit options and QR-based ticketing are operational across both networks, but fare structures remain separate based on the designated RRTS layout. You cannot use a standard DMRC Smart Card to pay RRTS fares — you need to purchase a separate RRTS ticket or use the NCRTC app.
Which platform should I stand on at Anand Vihar for Ghaziabad?
For the direct Blue Line route to Ghaziabad (Vaishali), go to Platform 1 at the Anand Vihar Metro Station. For the Namo Bharat high-speed rail towards Ghaziabad/Meerut, head to Platform 1 of the underground RRTS complex below the ISBT.
