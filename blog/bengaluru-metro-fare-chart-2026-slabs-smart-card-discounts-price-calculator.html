Bengaluru Metro Fare Chart 2026: Slabs, Smart Card Discounts & Price Calculator

MetroGuideIndia 2026-05-23 8 min read

Navigating Bengaluru’s traffic can be a chaotic challenge. Thankfully, Namma Metro (Bengaluru Metro) serves as a fast, reliable, and air-conditioned savior for millions of commuters across the city.

However, following the implementation of the annual automatic fare revision by the Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Limited (BMRCL), ticket prices across the 96-km operational network have undergone a slight change.

Whether you are a daily tech-corridor commuter or a tourist exploring the Garden City, this detailed guide breaks down the latest Bengaluru Metro fare structure, active discount slabs, and includes an interactive cost calculator to help plan your commute budget perfectly.

The New Bengaluru Metro Fare Structure (2026)

In compliance with the guidelines laid out by the First Fare Fixation Committee (FFC), BMRCL introduced a formula-based annual automatic fare revision. To insulate commuters from massive price shocks while keeping up with growing operational and electricity costs, the price adjustment has been strictly capped at 5%.

As a result, ticket prices have marginally increased by ₹1 to ₹5 depending on the distance you travel. The minimum fare is now ₹11 (up from ₹10), and the maximum fare is ₹95 (up from ₹90).

Smart Cards & NCMC: How to Slash Your Travel Costs

Even though the base ticket price has seen a marginal hike, BMRCL has retained all commuter-friendly discount metrics. If you use a Contactless Smart Card or a National Common Mobility Card (NCMC), you can significantly lower your daily commute expenses.

ℹ️
💡 Pro-Tip: The minimum balance required to pass through the entry gates on a Namma Metro Smart Card is ₹90. Keep your card topped up to avoid getting stopped at the turnstiles!

1. Time-Based Concessions

  • Peak Hours (5% Discount): You automatically save 5% on the base fare when travelling during rush hours using your Smart card or NCMC.
  • Off-Peak Hours (10% Discount): To encourage off-peak travel, BMRCL offers a generous 10% discount during the following windows on weekdays:
  • Start of service until 8:00 AM
  • 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM
  • 9:00 PM to closing time
  • Sundays & National Holidays (10% Discount): Enjoy a flat 10% discount all day long on Sundays and designated national holidays (January 26, August 15, and October 2).

2. Tourist & Unlimited Day Passes

If you are exploring Bangalore or planning multiple trips in a single day, buying a standard token can quickly add up. You can opt for revised Tourist Passes available at station counters:

  • 1-Day Pass: ₹263 via Mobile QR / ₹313 via Smart Card (Offers unlimited travel for 24 hours).
  • 3-Day Pass: ₹578 via Mobile QR / ₹628 via Smart Card.
  • 5-Day Pass: ₹893 via Mobile QR / ₹943 via Smart Card.

Summary: Quick Tips for Your Next Journey

  • Ditch the Lines: Skip the crowded ticket counter lines at busy hubs like Majestic (Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Station) or MG Road by purchasing digital QR tickets on the official Namma Metro mobile application or through WhatsApp.
  • Interchanges are Free: You can switch lines (e.g., Purple Line to Green Line at Majestic, or the newly opening Yellow line intersections) without paying any extra transaction or exit fees, provided you don't exit the station gates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) – Namma Metro Fares & Rules

Q1: Is there a separate or discounted ticket fare for children in Namma Metro?

No, BMRCL does not charge fares based on age. Tickets are strictly based on height. Children under 3 feet (90 cm) can travel completely free when accompanied by an adult. Anyone taller than 3 feet must purchase a full-fare ticket (token, QR code, or smart card).

Q2: What is the minimum balance required on a Namma Metro Smart Card?

The minimum balance required to enter the automatic station gates using a Contactless Smart Card or NCMC is ₹90. If your card balance falls below this limit, the turnstiles will not open, and you will need to top up your card at a counter or digital kiosk before entering.

Q3: What is the maximum time limit allowed inside the metro station network?

Once you tap your ticket or card at the entry gate, you have a maximum of 120 minutes (2 hours) to exit from any other station on the network. If you exceed this time limit, an overstay penalty fee of ₹10 per hour (capped up to a maximum of ₹50) will be charged at the exit gate.

If you enter and exit from the exact same station, you must complete your turnaround within 20 minutes to avoid paying a penalty.

Q4: Are tokens valid for multiple days if left unused?

No. A Single Journey Token or digital QR code ticket is only valid on the day of purchase. Furthermore, after buying a physical token, you must tap into the entry gates within 30 minutes at that specific station, or the token will expire and become invalid without a refund.

Q5: Can I get a refund on an unused or partially used Metro Smart Card?

BMRCL does not offer refunds on the remaining travel balance loaded onto your Smart Card. However, the card itself comes with a massive 10-year validity from the date of your last recharge, meaning your money stays safe for future trips if you take a break from commuting.

Q6: Can I buy a single ticket or QR code for a group of people?

Yes! If you are traveling in a group, you can generate a Group QR Ticket via the official Namma Metro mobile app or WhatsApp. A single group QR code can accommodate up to 6 passengers traveling together between the same two stations. Additionally, physical "Group Tickets" (paper tickets) are available at station counters for larger groups of 10 or more individuals at a discounted rate.
