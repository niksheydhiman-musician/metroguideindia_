Frequently Asked Questions
The estimated metro fare from Rajiv Chowk to New Delhi is ₹40 (Standard). Smart Card holders get approximately 10% discount. Fares are set by DMRC and subject to revision — verify on the official DMRC website before travel.
The estimated travel time from Rajiv Chowk to New Delhi by Delhi Metro is ~30 mins. This includes boarding time but may vary based on interchange waits and service frequency.
At Rajiv Chowk station, check the overhead platform indicators or ask station staff for the direction towards New Delhi. Platform numbers and directions are displayed inside the station concourse.
